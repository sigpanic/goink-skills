# Contributing to Goink Skills

Thanks for your interest in contributing a skill to Goink! This document explains what contributors should know before submitting a PR.

## 1. Originality & License

By submitting a pull request, you confirm that:

- You are the original author of the submitted content, or you have obtained authorization from the original author to contribute it;
- Your submission does not infringe any third party's copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property rights;
- Your submission does not plagiarize or copy large excerpts from others' works (writing methodology books, blog posts, course materials, etc.);
- You agree that the submitted content will be licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](LICENSE), and this license is irrevocable.

## 2. Content Policy

The following content will not be accepted:

- Content violating laws of China or your jurisdiction (pornography, violent incitement, politically sensitive material, defamation, etc.);
- Plagiarism or unauthorized copying of others' works;
- Skills that induce AI to generate infringing or illegal content;
- Advertisements, promotions, or commercial links.

Maintainers reserve the right to reject or remove any skill without explanation.

## 3. Submission Process

1. Fork this repository
2. Create a new `.md` file under `skills/` (see [.template/skill.md](.template/skill.md))
3. Fill in the YAML frontmatter:
   - Required: `name`, `description`, `category`, `mode` (must be `auto`; can switch to `manual`/`always` in App after download)
   - Optional: `author`, `version`
4. Open a pull request — CI will auto-check file paths and frontmatter format
5. Maintainers will review and merge

After merge, the index updates automatically and your skill becomes installable from within the Goink App.

## 4. Infringement Report

If you believe a skill in this repository infringes your copyright or other IP rights, please contact **2059869461@qq.com** with:

1. Your identity and contact information;
2. The original work and proof of ownership;
3. The skill filename and specific passages alleged to infringe;
4. A good faith statement that the use is unauthorized and you are authorized to act on behalf of the rights holder;
5. Your electronic (typed) signature.

The maintainer will respond within 7 business days and take down confirmed infringing content within 48 hours.

Skill content is provided by contributors. Maintainers only perform format review and make no warranty regarding the legality, non-infringement, accuracy, or fitness of any skill. Outputs generated by LLMs after skill injection are used at the user's own risk.
